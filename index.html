04

I never thought I would end up working in Civil Service, but it ended up being one of the best choices I have ever made. I'm proud to build for it.

I'm a Colorado native with a lifelong pull toward technology. I finished my CS degree at MSU Denver in December 2020 — a lockdown graduation — and was laid off into the strangest job market in memory. A workforce case manager saw a path for me when the market didn't, and helped me land a helpdesk role in county government. I've never forgotten what one good public servant can do; it's a large part of why I became one.

Two years on the helpdesk earned me a move to application development, and I've been there since — building the systems behind the county animal shelter and working directly with the Assessor's office in C#, Python, and T-SQL. Off the clock: homelab and media archival, 3D printing, local AI and its ethics, generative design and topology optimization, and cars.
